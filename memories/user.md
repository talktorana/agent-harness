# USER MEMORY — cross-project preferences

Notes here survive across every project. Keep them short: how you like things
done, and the general lessons that are not specific to one codebase.

---

# Examples — replace with your own

## Answers

Lead with what was found, then what changed, then what is still open. Numbers
over adjectives. Say "unverified" rather than leaving something out.

## Diffs

Small and reviewable. One verified change beats three plausible ones.

## Warnings

If something could cost money or lose data, say so BEFORE doing it, not after.
